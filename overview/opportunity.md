# The Opportunity

## The Cost of Starting Over

**Organizations routinely pay to reconstruct knowledge they already own.**

A production problem, customer requirement, or engineering change can send teams across documents, systems, and departments before they reach a trusted starting point. The records may exist, but the relevant context, prior decisions, and next responsibilities are often difficult to bring together.

The cost appears as lost expert time, slower execution, repeated investigations, inconsistent handoffs, and avoidable operational risk.

## Why AI Makes This More Urgent

Enterprise search and AI assistants have made information easier to find, summarize, and generate. Those are useful advances.

But producing an answer is not the same as getting work done. Teams still need to establish whether information is current, relevant to their situation, supported by reliable records, and suitable for the decision at hand. As AI-generated work expands, so can the effort needed to review and coordinate it.

**The next opportunity is to improve what happens between finding information and taking an informed action.**

## IKKA's Value Proposition

IKKA Solutions is developing enterprise software intended to make institutional knowledge more useful within everyday workflows.

The approach is designed to complement existing enterprise systems and AI tools—not replace the technology companies have already invested in.

We are focused on three outcomes:

- **More capacity for valuable work:** Spend less time rebuilding context and more time solving operational problems.
- **More consistent execution:** Bring the right knowledge, requirements, and responsibilities into decisions across teams.
- **Greater knowledge continuity:** Make prior experience and validated work easier to reuse rather than repeatedly rediscover.

## Where We Start

Engineering and regulated manufacturing provide a demanding proving ground. A delay or misunderstanding can affect a production line, product launch, quality event, or customer obligation.

Initial areas of customer discovery include manufacturing quality, new product introduction, and requirements-driven engineering workflows. These are areas for validation, not claims of completed customer deployments.

## How We Will Measure Value

The test is not how many questions an AI system can answer. It is whether a team can reach a **trusted next step** with less wasted effort.

Pilot outcomes may include time to a validated next action, hours spent searching and verifying information, repeated work avoided, and improved clarity of ownership or outstanding requirements. Results will be established through customer-specific baselines and evaluation.

**Better access to knowledge is useful. Better organizational performance is the opportunity.**
