# Industrial Pilot Collaboration

### One costly workflow. A bounded pilot. Measurable results.

IKKA is seeking a small number of industrial and engineering teams interested in evaluating a recurring operational problem where people spend too much time collecting information, validating context, or coordinating the next action.

The objective is straightforward: **test whether existing organizational knowledge can help a team reach a trusted decision or next step with less effort.**

## What Makes a Good Pilot?

A suitable engagement has:

- **One defined workflow** with a recurring, material business problem.
- **An accountable business owner** who can describe today's process and the desired outcome.
- **Approved information and stakeholders** available for a limited evaluation.
- **A measurable baseline**, such as time to the next approved action, review effort, or repeated work.

Illustrative areas include quality investigations, launch readiness, and requirements-driven engineering work. The actual pilot scope is determined with the participating organization.

## How We Work Together

| Step | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Select** | Agree on one problem worth solving. |
| **Map** | Understand the current workflow, handoffs, information, and approvals. |
| **Pilot** | Evaluate a focused IKKA-supported workflow using agreed information and boundaries. |
| **Measure** | Compare outcomes against the baseline and decide whether further development is warranted. |

Engagements are founder-led and scoped individually. A six-to-eight-week evaluation is a possible starting point, not a fixed commitment. Scope, costs, access, deliverables, and success criteria are agreed before work begins.

## Experience Behind the Work

IKKA is founded on approximately a decade of industrial engineering experience across automotive sensors, semiconductors, advanced materials, product development, manufacturing, and quality systems.

## Confidentiality and Next Steps

Only information explicitly approved by the participating organization is used. Data handling, confidentiality, access permissions, and intellectual-property boundaries are agreed before sharing sensitive material. An appropriate NDA or pilot agreement can formalize those terms.
